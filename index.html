About Me

Lingyan Ran received his B.S. and Ph.D. degrees from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), Xi'an, China, in 2011 and 2018. Earlier, he was a visiting scholar at Stevens Institute of Technology (SIT), Hoboken, NJ, from 2013 to 2015. He is currently a Professor with the School of Computer Science, NWPU. His research focuses on aerospace intelligent computing for Earth observation, including remote sensing image interpretation (semantic segmentation and change detection), low-altitude UAV perception in open and cross-domain environments, label-efficient and continual learning, and efficient perception models and agents for resource-constrained aerial and on-board platforms, including domestic computing hardware. He serves as a Guest Editor of Remote Sensing and a Young Editorial Board Member of Intelligence & Robotics. He is a member of China Computer Federation (CCF), China Society of Image and Graphics (CSIG), and Chinese Association of Automation (CAA).
